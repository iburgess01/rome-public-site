Rome / Data handling

Privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026

Rome is a personal OpenClaw-based assistant operated by its owner for personal use. This policy describes its Google newsletter integration and this public information website. The Google integration is used only with the owner’s account.

Google data accessed

Rome retrieves selected Gmail newsletter messages, including their contents, headers, links, message identifiers and labels. It reads an owner-selected Google Sheets preferences document and may read related Google Drive file information needed for that workflow.

Purpose and permitted changes

Rome uses this information to find unread newsletters, rank and summarise relevant stories, create digests, and track which messages were processed. After saving and verifying a digest and its processing record, it removes the unread label from the exact successfully processed messages and checks the result. The workflow does not send, draft, delete or archive email, or edit Google Sheets or Drive files.

AI processing and service providers

Selected newsletter content, preferences and relevant conversation context are sent to OpenAI to generate the digest. Google supplies the authorised Gmail, Sheets and Drive API access. These providers process information under the terms and settings applicable to the owner’s accounts. This policy does not promise that all processing remains on the owner’s computer or that providers retain no data.

Rome does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Its Google-data use is limited to the personal newsletter features described here. Rome’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Digests, message identifiers, processing records and conversation/tool history are stored in the owner’s private Rome environment. That environment is protected by access controls and disk encryption, and its recovery backups are encrypted. Saved records remain until the owner deletes them; there is no automatic per-message deletion period promised here. Copies in recovery backups may remain until those backups are rotated or deleted. Provider-side retention is governed separately by the relevant service terms and account settings.

Control and deletion

The owner can stop the newsletter automation and revoke Rome’s access in Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorised API access but does not erase digests, history or backups already created. The owner can delete those copies separately.

This public website

This static information site uses GitHub Pages for hosting. Its source adds no analytics, advertising, cookies, forms or Google sign-in. The hosting provider may process standard request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to serve and protect the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Private newsletters, account credentials and generated digests are not published on this site.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when Rome’s data practices change. Any new Google-data use will be disclosed before it is introduced, with renewed consent where needed.
